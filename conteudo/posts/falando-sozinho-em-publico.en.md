I have always been passionate about teaching. I remember writing summaries for my classmates in a class newspaper I ran with a friend back in 2008.

In 2021, when I started working as a tester at my first job, I created an [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/devguara/) page to teach what I was learning as my career moved forward. The project never went beyond that, but I plan to bring the page back soon. More than five years and many ups and downs later, here I am, writing my third post!

## Talking to myself in public

A few years ago, I saw a Facebook post comparing using social media to "talking to yourself in public". The idea was that many people share memes and posts without necessarily interacting with anyone: they just express something about themselves online, without caring much about who is watching.

The posts on this site follow a similar line. What I write here are my own learning notes about software development, mixed with the things I like, and I let other people read them.

## Focus on the path

My only goal is my own learning. I don't intend to become a programming influencer or anything like that; I believe these posts will be read only by me and a small handful of recruiters.

## Motivation

As I said at the start, the seed of the idea was always there: I have been passionate about education since I was a kid. Even though I don't feel ready, for now, to teach in a formal way, I believe I have a lot to share, and a lot to learn too.

The push to finally get the idea off the ground came from [an explanation by Fabio Akita on Flow Podcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N3eU-2VIByQ) (in Portuguese) about why he keeps a [blog](https://akitaonrails.com/): how easy it makes revisiting things he has already learned.

Another motivation is the so-called Feynman technique, which suggests learning a subject by explaining it in simple words, as if you were teaching someone else. That is why not everything I write here is something I have mastered: most posts will follow my own progress as I learn new things. Don't take everything on this site as absolute truth.

## What to expect from here on

I plan to publish at least one post a week, about the projects I am building and what I have learned from them. I also want to write about other topics related to programming and corporate environments.
