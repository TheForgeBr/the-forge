Hi, my name is Rhuan Marques, and this is my first post!

The forge is my digital space for presenting personal projects and what I have learned from them. The idea was strongly inspired by the blog [Akita on Rails](https://akitaonrails.com/), and it is meant to work as a digital notebook, where I write down insights from my studies and what I learned from them.

## What is the forge?

The forge is a personal development "seal": it works as a mark showing that a product was made following a standard of production and quality. The idea came up in 2021 with a group of developer friends. At first, the forge would be a quality seal for a team of devs, each with their own insignia, but I was the only one who carried on with the idea.

![The old forge seal: a fist raising a hammer against an orange background with red rays, and a Tengwar inscription below](selo-antigo.webp 'The forge seal in its early years')

The idea of a forge comes from an elf in the universe of _The Lord of the Rings_ (my favorite work): Fëanor, lord of the Noldor. Other inspirations were the _Devil Forgemasters_ from the Castlevania game and visual concepts from the game _Magic: The Gathering_.

![A lit forge in an Elvish stone hall, with orange smoke turning into blue magic as it reaches crystal screens showing code](forja.webp 'The forge, on the home page of the site')

## Philosophy

All the projects here came from a list I have been keeping in my phone's notes app since 2022. I believe each idea I have had is an expression of my creativity through my development skills, like an artisan's signature on a forged piece.

## The Blacksmith

I have been working in software development since 2021. I have a degree in Systems Analysis and Development from the Catholic University of Brasília (UCB), and I also studied Software Engineering at the University of Brasília (UnB). Most of my experience has been as a mobile and frontend developer, always with the React and TypeScript stack.

I consider myself a creative and curious person, with a passion for learning multidisciplinary concepts and applying them to everyday solutions. Since I work with programming, most of my ideas end up as software solutions.

## The development process

With the popularization and improvement of development processes using AI tools, I found a viable way to get my projects off the ground.

Every project follows the same development process:

1. Ideation
2. Research of sources and requirements
3. Brainstorm
4. Requirements writing
5. Planning
6. Breaking the work into stages
7. Development
8. Validation
9. Deployment

The development flow blends Agile, Kanban and XP, all adapted to solo development based on AI.
